Philips Domestic Appliances launches first all-in-one air purifier with AI technology

Breathe life into your home with the Philips Air Performer 3-in-1 Purifier, Fan & Heater that automatically cleans, cools and heats.

  • The Philips Air Performer is the first multi-functional air purifier in the world to use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to automatically clean the air, warm it up or cool you down
  • Its HEPA filtration system filters 99.97% of unseen particles1 and thoroughly purifies rooms up to 70 m22
  • Connected app Air+ controls & monitors indoor and outdoor air quality.

Amsterdam, the Netherlands: Philips Domestic Appliances introduces the Philips Air Performer 3-in-1 Purifier, Fan & Heater. The machine has been created to be a performance powerhouse, encompassing maximum purification, powerful fan cooling, and heating within one convenient home appliance. The Philips Air Performer has a 3-layer NanoProtect HEPA filtration system that eliminates 99.97% of all unseen particles1 from the air passing through the product. The air purifier uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) to automatically clean the air and adjust to its user’s comfort through smart sensing and machine learning. The AI-function can be turned on or left off, whatever is preferred. The Philips Air Performer also comes with the connected Air+ for added convenience.

Henk S. de Jong, CEO at Philips Domestic Appliances: “At Philips Domestic Appliances we help consumers to turn their houses into homes by creating meaningful innovations that make their lives better and easier. The Philips Air Performer is a product that does just that. It is the first multi-functional air purifier in the world to use Artificial Intelligence to automatically clean the air, warm it up or cool you down. This innovation is designed with energy and cost efficiency in mind. Thanks to advanced sensors and smart algorithms, the motor only runs when needed and warms up the room up to 30 times3 faster than a traditional gas radiator. After extensive consumer research and technical development, I’m looking forward for this connected innovation to reach people all around the world allowing them to enjoy cleaner and healthier air at home.”

Cleanse any living space like never before The Philips Air Performer has a 3-layer NanoProtect HEPA filtration system with active carbon and pre-filter, which removes 99.97% of all unseen particles, filtering pollutants as small as 0.003 microns1, smaller than the smallest known virus. It removes viruses, bacteria, pollen, dust, pet dander, dust mites, harmful gases, odors and other pollutants. The Air Performer thoroughly purifies rooms up to 70 m22 allowing people to enjoy healthier air in their home all year long.

Introducing AI technology to the world of air purification The Philips Air Performer is the first multi-functional air purifier in the world to use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to automatically clean the air, warm it up or cool you down. Thanks to Philips AI-powered Self-Adaptive technology, it does not only sense the air quality4 but also analyzes outdoor data, room size and learns from usage patterns to maximize performance while minimizing noise and energy consumption.

Pure and cozy air all year round The Philips Air Performer creates streams of purified air that thoroughly sweep through both small and large rooms via a bladeless design that also heats or cools. With its patented Dual Flow Engine, consumers can choose between direct airflow for cooling, heating, or diffused airflow for optimal air circulation. In the summer, it cools you down with powerful clean airflow that feels natural and pleasant. And in winter, the Philips Air Performer heats up in 3 seconds; providing warmth to the room in no time with its efficient ceramic technology. Ensuring optimum comfort, the Philips Air Performer also comes with a Sleep mode, that provides an ultra-quiet operation system that is quieter than a whisper.

Connected with renewed Air+ app The Philips Air Performer can connect to WiFi and comes with the Air+ app, which monitors indoor and outdoor pollutants and keeps the user in control, at home or away, with a remote control and notifications about their air concerns. Thanks to its smart sensors, it detects and displays in real-time PM2.5, allergens, harmful gases, temperature and humidity. Download the app for iOS here and for Android here.

1 From the air that passes through the filter, tested with NaCl aerosol by a 3rd party laboratory 2 Calculated according to NRCC-54013 test standard, using cigarette smoke CADR tested according to GB/T18801-2015. Air Performer has a CADR of 270 m2. 3 Simulation with a single room 30m3, 18C to 20C at 1.5m level, Air Performer vs. central heating (based on gas boiler). 4The device has 6 sensor outputs: Fine Particles (PM2.5), Gases (VOC), Allergens, Temperature, Humidity, and Light Control.
